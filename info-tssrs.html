The Summary

What is TSSRS?

The Technical Support Service Request System (TSSRS) is a web-based platform developed for the Iloilo City Government to streamline the management of technical support requests. It enables every offices accross the City Hall to submit and track service requests while allowing administrators and technicians to efficiently assign, monitor, and resolve reported issues through a centralized workflow.

By replacing manual processes with a digital system, TSSRS improves communication, accountability, and response times while providing organized records that help the City Management Information Systems Office deliver faster and more reliable technical support.

Chapter 03

Mapping the System

My first step was understanding how requests moved through the organization. I identified the different people involved, their responsibilities, and the information they needed at each stage of the process. I began tracing the journey of a request.

"Who creates it?"

"Who reviews it?"

"Who acts on it?"

"Who reports on it?"

The more I mapped the workflow, the more I realized I wasn't designing screens.

I was designing a system of responsibilities.

I mapped how a request was created, reviewed, assigned, completed, and eventually reported. This helped me see the workflow as a system rather than a collection of individual tasks.

Chapter 05

Built for Every Role

The system allows users to submit requests, track their progress, assign technicians, update request statuses, and generate reports.

Different user roles were implemented so each participant in the workflow could access the tools and information relevant to their responsibilities.

01
Submit Requests
Users Submit a service request through the portal for any device repair.
02
Track Progress
Users Admin Technician Follow a request's status in real time from submission to assignment to resolution. Each role sees only the tools and data relevant to their responsibilities.
03
Assign Technicians
Admin Route incoming requests to the right technician based on workload, availability, and skill.
04
Update Statuses
Technician Technicians log progress, updates, and issues so requesters and admins always see the current state. Technicians can also message users directly through the Built-In Messaging System.
05
Generate Reports
Admin Every service request feeds detailed volume, turnaround time, and technician resolution data into the dashboard for reporting.
06
Equipment Return and Completion
Users Admin Administrators will notify users for service request completion and process the returning of the equipment.
